The world is in an era where we have access to all the information known to humankind, yet we show signs of being “dumber than ever", says Sky News host Chris Kenny.

“That is the great paradox of our time,” he said.

“And I offer as my latest bit of evidence a quiz program from Britain, it’s called Tipping Point and Nine Media runs it here in Australia.

“Have a look at this question and marvel at the answer … a question about Homer, the ancient Greek poet who gave us the Illiad and the Odyssey, and yet you get answers relating to his namesake, a cartoon character.

“Yeah, easy to confuse, remember all those epic poems in the Simpsons?”
