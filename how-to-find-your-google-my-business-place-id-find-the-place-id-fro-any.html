Find the Place ID fro any Google My Business Listing.

1. Bring up the Google My Business Knowledge Panel for the listing by searching for the business name in Google

2. Open the page source code... (usually by right clicking → view page source)

3. Search for "CHIJ" , or "data-pid=" in the source code.
