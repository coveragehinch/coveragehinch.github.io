In Britain "How Can I Be Sure" was a #1 hit for two weeks in the singles charts being the second of Cassidy's overall 11 UK chart hits six of which would reach the Top Ten including his second #1 UK hit: the double A-side hit "Daydreamer"/"The Puppy Song" (#1 for three weeks). "How Can I Be Sure" also charted with more moderate impact in Australia (#16), Canada (#22), Germany (#33), and the Netherlands (#13).

He won Best All Around Performer and Best Singer awards for his 3 year stint at the MGM in Las Vegas for EFX.
