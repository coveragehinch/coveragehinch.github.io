It said it plans to begin the approval process for CimetrA in India before rolling it out to a total of five countries in central and eastern Europe and central Asia.

CimetrA is a pharmaceutical synthetic composition of Curcumin and Boswellia that is thought to have an anti-inflammatory effect as well as potentially modulating the immune system.
