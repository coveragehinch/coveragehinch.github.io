What does Inauspicious mean? Learn the meaning of Inauspicious as we define this advanced vocabulary word with a simple definition, pictures, example sentences, English pronunciation and audio.

Inauspicious definition (adjective) unlucky; not likely to be successful

Inauspicious pronunciation: in-aw-spish-uh s

Examples of Inauspicious in a sentence:

1. As I looked at the dark clouds in the sky, I knew today was an inauspicious day for a picnic.

2. Jill was unhappy when the fortune-teller told her she was destined to be inauspicious in love.

3. The restaurant owner decided to close his café after a few inauspicious months.

4. Since I hit a car backing out of my driveway, I would definitely describe my first driving experience as inauspicious.

5. When William overslept and missed an important meeting at work, he had an inauspicious day that began with him being screamed at by his boss.
