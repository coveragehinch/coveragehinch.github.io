The words "less" and "fewer" are often confused by english speakers and used improperly. Even people who have spoken english for their entire lives make this mistake when describing quantities and amounts. We will discuss the proper uses of the words "less" and "fewer" as well as the reasons behind those proper uses so that you can use the correct words every time.
