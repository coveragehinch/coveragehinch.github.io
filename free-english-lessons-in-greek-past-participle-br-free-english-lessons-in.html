In this video, we look at how we form the Past Participle in English and where it is used. In English, we have two kinds of verbs: Regular Verbs and Irregular Verbs. We form the Past Simple Tense and the Past Participle of Regular Verbs by adding -ed at the end of the infinitive. The Past Simple Tense and the Past Participle of Irregular Verbs have different forms of their own which we have to learn by heart. If you want to learn most of them easily click here:

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HP2WQ_6y3wQ

We use the Past Participle to form all Simple Perfect Tenses in English and the Passive forms of the verbs (Passive Voice). Sometimes, some Past Participles of certain verbs can be used as adjectives before nouns.
