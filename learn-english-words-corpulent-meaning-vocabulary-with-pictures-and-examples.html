What does Corpulent mean? Learn the meaning of Corpulent as we define this advanced vocabulary word with a simple definition, pictures, example sentences, English pronunciation and audio.

Corpulent definition (adjective) having a large bulky body

Corpulent pronunciation: kawr-pyuh-luh nt

Examples of Corpulent in a sentence:

1. Isn’t it amusing how a man as corpulent as Santa Claus can fit through a tiny chimney?

2. If I stopped eating so much junk food and exercised more, I would be less corpulent.

3. The foolish mailman tried to fit the corpulent package in the tiny mailbox.

4. Because the piano is too corpulent to fit through the front door, we will have to bring it in through the garage.

5. Frank used an editing program to give himself a less corpulent look in the photograph.
