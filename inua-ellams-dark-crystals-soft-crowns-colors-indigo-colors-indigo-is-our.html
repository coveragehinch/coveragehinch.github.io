COLORS INDIGO is our brand new range of apparel inspired by the transition from night into day, the deep blues of winter skies, and expanses of sea on sprawling coastlines. ⁠
⁠
To introduce the collection, Nigeria-born, U.K.-based poet, performer, playwright, graphic artist, and designer Inua Ellams delivered a dramatic spoken-word performance of ‘Dark Crystals / Soft Crowns’. Written especially for the project, the poem is inspired by his own interpretation of the color indigo, as well as his personal heritage.⁠
