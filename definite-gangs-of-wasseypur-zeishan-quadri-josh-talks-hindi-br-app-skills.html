Zeishan Quadri aka Definite Khan from Gangs of Wasseypur has had a life unlike any others. Gangs of Wasseypur did not begin as an Anurag Kashyap vision or any sort of collaborative project on unraveling crime in Bihar. The story owes its thanks entirely to Zeishan Qadri’s life experience, and it would never have been realized, had he found a stable career or started his own business, like he had planned. That nothing really materialized for him and he came to Mumbai on a comical hunch that he could make it in films was the beginning of his success story. He recounts his thoroughly entertaining journey. Zeishan also talks about life after Gangs of Wasseypur and what he now plans to do in Bollywood.
